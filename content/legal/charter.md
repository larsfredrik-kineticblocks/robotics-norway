## A. Public charter (English original)

From the founding meeting slides, 5 November 2026, Startuplab, Oslo. Slide title: CHARTER / WHAT ROBOTICS NORWAY WILL BE.

Robotics Norway is the network/cluster for Norwegian companies that build robots, physical AI and the data and software around them, and gives its members a shared route to buyers, partners, talent and investors at home and abroad.

### Open membership
Open to robotics companies and organisations in Norway, and to individuals who live in Norway or work for a Norwegian robotics company or in academia.

### Run by its members
Members elect the steering group and take turns as administrator, one year at a time.

### 500 robot labs
Five (hundred) labs in Norwegian cities where members test, collect data and demonstrate, starting at Startuplab.

### Study trips
Joint trips to Odense, San Francisco and Shenzhen to meet clusters, buyers and manufacturers together.

### A door to the Bay Area
A funded project, led by Innovation Norway, that introduces Norwegian robotics companies to the Bay Area.

### Trust between members
Market information shared in the network stays confidential, and every member runs its own business freely.
