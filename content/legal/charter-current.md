# Charter for Robotics Norway

**Status:** Draft · 6 November 2026 · For the steering group  
**Founding meeting:** 5 November 2026, Startuplab, Oslo

This charter sets out the purpose, membership, organisation and activities of the Robotics Norway business network. Where earlier invitations or presentations mention membership fees or a central network budget, the rules in this charter apply.

---

## § 1 Purpose

Robotics Norway is the business network for companies that build robots, physical AI, and the data and software around them. The network gives members a shared route to customers, partners, talent and investors in Norway and abroad.

The network shall be easy to join, complementary to other robotics networks and clusters, and run by its members.

---

## § 2 Membership

1. The network is open to all robotics companies in Norway.  
2. Individuals may also become members if they live in Norway, or work for a Norwegian robotics company or at a Norwegian university, university college or research institute.  
3. The network may open for international members by decision of the steering group.  
4. Membership is free. The network has no budget and charges no membership fee.  
5. A company becomes a member by joining the network’s email list and accepting that its logo, contact person and company information are published on the network’s website.  
6. An individual becomes a member in the same way and accepts that their name, and their affiliation if they give one, are published on the network’s website. Their contact details are not published.  
7. A member leaves by notifying the administrator. Withdrawal takes effect when the administrator has confirmed receipt, and the member’s information is removed from the website within a reasonable time.  
8. Membership in Robotics Norway does not preclude simultaneous membership in other networks or clusters.

---

## § 3 Organisation

1. The network is governed by a steering group composed of volunteers among the members.  
2. The steering group sets priorities, coordinates activities and adopts amendments to this charter.  
3. The network leader and the administration rotate and each term lasts one year.  
4. Leader for 2027 is Lars-Fredrik Forberg, CEO of Kinetic Blocks. Kinetic Blocks AS is the administrator company in 2027. The steering group appoints the leader and administrator for subsequent years.  
5. No member may bind the other members without a mandate from the steering group.

*At the founding meeting on 5 November 2026 the following were confirmed for the steering group: Daniel (Saro Robotics), Eva (Necia), Christian Rokseth (Humanoid.guide) and Runway FBU. Others may join by contacting the administrator.*

---

## § 4 Activities

1. **Network meetings.** The network shall arrange an unspecified number of network meetings each year.  
2. **Collaboration.** The network shall collaborate with other robotics networks and clusters in Norway and abroad.  
3. **Study trips.** The network shall arrange an unspecified number of study trips each year. These are financed by the members who take part.  
4. **Projects.** The network shall establish, seek funding for and carry out specific projects for relevant members. Such projects are financed by the member companies that take part, and where applicable by public grants. Participation is voluntary and creates no obligations for other members.  
5. **Robot labs.** The network shall support the establishment of robot labs in Norway, but shall not finance them. Labs have their own budgets and their own funding applications.

---

## § 5 Finances

1. Robotics Norway as a network is free to join and has no central budget.  
2. Costs of study trips are covered by the members who take part.  
3. Costs of specific projects are covered by the member companies that take part in the project, and where applicable by grants sought for that project.  
4. Robot labs are not financed by the network.

---

## § 6 Digital assets

1. The network owns the domain **roboticsnorway.org**.  
2. The website shall display member companies’ logos, contact persons and company information, and individual members’ names, in accordance with § 2.  
3. The administrator manages the domain and website on behalf of the network during their term of office, and hands over access to the next administrator.

---

## § 7 Trust between members

1. Market information shared in the network shall be treated as confidential unless the member who shares it states otherwise.  
2. Each member runs its own business freely, including with customers and partners contacted through the network.

---

## § 8 Amendments

Amendments to this charter are adopted by the steering group. Amendments to § 2 items 1–7 and § 5 (membership and a free network with no budget) require clear consent from the members, not only a simple majority of the steering group.
