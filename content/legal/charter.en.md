**These texts are translations, not signed originals.** The source-language documents are in `charter.md` sections B and C. The public charter in `charter.md` section A is already in English (taken from the founding-meeting slides) and is not re-translated here.

## Letter of intent on establishing the Robotics Norway business network

*Translation of «Intensjonsavtale om etablering av bedriftsnettverket Robotics Norway». Draft 28 October 2026 for signing at the founding meeting at Startuplab on 5 November 2026.*

### 1 Parties

This letter of intent is entered into between the companies listed in appendix 1, each referred to as a “Participant” and together as the “Participants”, with Kinetic Blocks AS, organisation number 938 345 627, as administrator company for project year 2027.

### 2 Purpose

The Participants intend to establish the Robotics Norway business network in order to develop and realise new market opportunities in robotics and physical AI in Norway and internationally, based on the Participants’ existing products, services, competence and technology, and they have a shared need for access to buyers, partners, competence and investors.

### 3 Application and administration

The Participants authorise Kinetic Blocks AS to apply to Innovation Norway by 1 December 2026 for support for a main project under the business-network scheme for project year 2027, with a budget of NOK 350,000 per year, in which joint study trips to Odense in January, San Francisco in April and Shenzhen in October 2027 are a central part of the network’s activities. The administrator role rotates among the Participants for one project year at a time, and the steering group elects the administrator company for subsequent years.

### 4 Participants’ contributions in the application phase

Each Participant appoints a contact person and a project participant and sends the administrator company, by 20 November 2026, a short company presentation and a declaration of de minimis aid received, so that the application can be submitted by the deadline.

### 5 Robot labs

The Participants intend to plan robot labs in Norwegian cities that are open to the network’s members, with the first lab at Startuplab in Oslo, and the labs shall have their own budget and their own funding applications.

### 6 Relation to the cooperation agreement

This letter of intent creates no financial obligations. The Participants’ rights and duties, including cash contributions, governance, ownership of results and confidentiality, are set out in the cooperation agreement, which is signed before the project starts on 1 January 2027 if Innovation Norway awards a grant.

### 7 Duration

This letter of intent applies from signing until the cooperation agreement is signed, or until it is clear that no grant will be awarded for project year 2027.

### Signature

Oslo, 5 November 2026
For Kinetic Blocks AS: ____________________
For [company]: ____________________

### Appendix

1 Participant list with organisation number, contact person and project participant

## Cooperation agreement for the Robotics Norway business network

*Translation of «Samarbeidsavtale for bedriftsnettverket Robotics Norway». Draft 28 October 2026, based on Innovation Norway’s checklist for cooperation agreements in business networks. The parties themselves must adapt the draft and consider the need for legal advice.*

### 1 Parties

The agreement is entered into between the following companies, each a “Participant” and together the “Participants”:

- Kinetic Blocks AS, organisation number 938 345 627
- [Company], organisation number [___]
- [Company], organisation number [___]
- [Company], organisation number [___]

The administrator role rotates among the Participants under clause 4, and Kinetic Blocks AS is project owner and administrator company for project year 2027 and applies for a grant on behalf of the network for that year. The project leader is [name], [company]. The contact person and project participant for each Participant appear in appendix 1.

### 2 Background and purpose

The Participants have entered into a letter of intent dated 5 November 2026 with a view to establishing the Robotics Norway business network. The purpose of the collaboration is increased growth and value creation for the Participants by developing and realising new market opportunities in robotics and physical AI in Norway and in the United States, particularly in the San Francisco Bay Area and Boston, based on the Participants’ existing products, services, competence and technology. The collaboration is based on the Participants’ shared need for access to buyers, partners, competence and investors, and on complementary resources that provide a basis for synergies.

### 3 The project and its scope

The project covers a shared point of contact towards US companies, research environments and investors; meetings and events in Norway and the US; joint study trips to Odense in January, San Francisco in April and Shenzhen in October 2027; exchange of market information among the Participants; cooperation with Stanford and NTNU; joint input to public funding bodies and to industry and research programmes; and planning of robot labs in Norwegian cities that are open to the members. The robot labs will have their own budget and funding applications, and only the planning is included in the project’s cost base. The strategy and implementation plan for the project is appendix 2.
The project does not cover the Participants’ other business activities. Each Participant remains free to run its own business and enter into agreements with others, including customers and partners that the network is in contact with, subject to the limits in clause 12.

### 4 Legal form and organisation

The project is not placed in a separate company and is carried out with the administrator company as project owner. The administrator company applies for the grant, receives and manages the grant and cash contributions, enters into supplier agreements on behalf of the network within the adopted budget and action plan, and has the main responsibility for implementation. Agreements outside the adopted budget or that bind the Participants beyond the project period require a decision by the steering group. No Participant may bind the other Participants without written authority. The administrator role rotates among the Participants for one project year at a time. Kinetic Blocks AS holds the role in 2027, and the steering group elects the administrator company for the following project year no later than three months before the application for that year is to be submitted. Each administrator company is responsible for the application, accounts and reporting for its own project year, and the outgoing administrator transfers the project accounts, unused funds, the basis for invoicing cash contributions, and documentation to the incoming administrator at the end of the project year.

### 5 Management, steering group and project leader

The project is led by a steering group with [number] members elected from the Participants’ representatives, in which the administrator company at any time has one permanent member. The steering group adopts the strategy, annual action plan and budget, approves the admission of new Participants and follows up implementation. The steering group is quorate when a majority of the members are present, and decisions are taken by simple majority. In the event of a tie, the chair has a casting vote. Changes to the budget, cash contributions or this agreement require unanimity among the Participants.
The project leader is responsible for day-to-day operations in line with the action plan, reports to the steering group and prepares applications and reports to Innovation Norway. The project leader may be engaged externally if the steering group finds it necessary for capacity or competence.

### 6 Participants’ contributions and obligations

Each Participant undertakes to:

- provide a named project participant with time set aside for meetings, events and project work, with the commitment anchored at management level;
- pay the agreed cash contribution to the administrator company, see clause 9;
- submit a de minimis aid declaration and notify the administrator company if its own ceiling has been exceeded;
- contribute competence, contacts and market information in line with the action plan;
- cover its own hours and its own travel expenses, which are not part of the project’s cost base, except for the network’s joint study trips to San Francisco, Odense and Shenzhen that are included in the adopted budget.

The administrator company contributes matching self-financing in the form of funding from its operations. The scope is described in the application and the project report.

### 7 Ownership and rights

Each Participant retains ownership of competence, products, methods, intellectual property, equipment and distribution networks that it brings into the project, and no Participant obtains a right to use another’s background rights without a separate written agreement. Results developed jointly within the project, such as reports, market analyses, contact lists and the network’s name and profile, are owned by the Participants jointly and may be used freely by each Participant in its own business. Results that one Participant develops alone are owned by that Participant.

### 8 Activities, phases and milestones

The project is carried out in three annual phases from 1 January 2027. Each phase has an action plan with activities, a responsible Participant and milestones, adopted by the steering group, see appendix 4 for year 1. Important milestones are:

- a signed letter of intent and establishment of the network at the founding meeting at Startuplab on 5 November 2026;
- company presentations and de minimis declarations from all Participants by 20 November 2026, and the application to Innovation Norway for year 1 by 1 December 2026;
- a signed cooperation agreement before project start;
- an event at Nordic Innovation House in Palo Alto before Christmas 2026 as a preparatory activity;
- applications for years 2 and 3 well before the current project period expires.

### 9 Financing

The project has a budget of NOK 350,000 per year and is financed by a grant from Innovation Norway and cash contributions from the Participants. The grant is given as de minimis aid and is allocated among all Participants, including the administrator company. Approved costs not covered by the grant are covered by cash contributions allocated according to the key in appendix 3, which is based on annual rates of NOK 1,000 for pre-seed startups, NOK 5,000 for other companies and NOK 20,000 for large companies. A Participant without remaining de minimis headroom pays a higher cash contribution equal to its share of the grant.
Cash contributions are invoiced by the administrator company [quarterly/semi-annually] according to the adopted budget. The steering group ensures that the project at all times has sufficient liquidity. Information about other public support for the project is given in the application.

### 10 Accounts and reporting

The administrator company is responsible for budgeting, accounting and reporting. The project accounts include only costs booked at the administrator company, and booked costs must in total be at least as large as the grant. The administrator company presents accounts to the steering group every six months and at the end of the project. The scope of the Participants’ effort, such as participation in meetings and events, is described in the final report without a requirement for timesheets. The project has no income at the outset. Any income, for example participation fees at events, goes to the project and reduces the need for cash contributions.

### 11 Information and communication

The project leader keeps the Participants informed about activities, finances and decisions. External communication on behalf of Robotics Norway is done by the project leader or by a person designated by the steering group. No Participant may speak on behalf of the network without such authority. Each Participant may state that it is a participant in the network.

### 12 Confidential information

The Participants have a duty of confidentiality regarding all information and material they obtain in connection with the collaboration that is not generally known, including market information, prices and customer relationships that other Participants share. The duty of confidentiality continues after the agreement has ended or the Participant has withdrawn. The steering group may require project participants and external contractors to sign a confidentiality declaration.

### 13 Admission and withdrawal of Participants

New Participants may be admitted by decision of the steering group, provided that Norwegian small and medium-sized companies still make up at least 50 percent of the Participants and that the new Participant accedes to this agreement in writing. New Participants pay cash contributions from the date of admission.
A Participant may withdraw at the end of a project year with [three] months’ written notice. Cash contributions already paid are not refunded, and the Participant covers its share of commitments entered into before withdrawal. The duty of confidentiality under clause 12 continues to apply.

### 14 Default

If a Participant materially breaches the agreement, including by failing to pay the cash contribution, the steering group may after written notice exclude the Participant from further collaboration. If a breach causes financial loss, ordinary rules on damages apply.

### 15 Duration

The agreement applies from signing through the project period of up to three years from 1 January 2027. If Innovation Norway does not award a grant for a project year, the steering group decides whether the project shall continue with other financing or be closed. The agreement may be extended by written agreement among the Participants.

### 16 Governing law and disputes

The agreement is governed by Norwegian law. Disputes shall first be sought resolved amicably. If that fails, each Participant may bring the dispute before the ordinary courts, with Oslo District Court as the venue.

### Signature

The agreement is executed in [number] copies, one for each Participant, or signed electronically.

### Appendices

1. Participant list with organisation number, contact person and project participant
2. Three-year strategy and implementation plan
3. Budget, financing plan and allocation key for cash contributions
4. Action plan for year 1
