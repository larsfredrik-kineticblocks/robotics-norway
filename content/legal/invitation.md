The document index gathered on 6 November 2026 records that this file is shared so anyone with the link can view it, and that it was also shared with additional named readers, including shifter.redaksjonen@schibsted.com and kundesupport@innovasjonnorge.no.

The public charter from the founding meeting is published on this website. The full invitation remains in the Google Doc.
