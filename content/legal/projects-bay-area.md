# Project: A door to the Bay Area

Short description for the Projects tab, compiled 6 November 2026 (Europe/Oslo) from the founding-meeting slides and Granola notes. Not a formal signed project document.

**A door to the Bay Area** is a funded internationalisation project, separate from but anchored in the open Robotics Norway member network. It introduces a smaller group of Norwegian robotics companies (slides: 3–10; Innovation Norway discussion: 5–10, with Bay Area as a primary market) to buyers, partners and investors in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Planned elements described in the materials:

- A shared “door opener” rather than each company building the same US contacts alone.
- Preparatory event before Christmas 2026 / late 2026 at Nordic Innovation House in Palo Alto or at Stanford (Stanford was recommended in the 29 Oct 2026 Innovation Norway SF check-in; the invitation still names NIH for premises).
- Study trip to San Francisco in April 2027, plus buyer meetings through the year.
- Innovation Norway (San Francisco office) proposed as paid project manager / on-the-ground door opener under Innovation Norway’s *bedriftsnettverk* scheme. Grant discussed as up to about NOK 1 million per year for up to three years; a member company must be the legal applicant (Robotics Norway as such cannot apply). Kinetic Blocks AS is proposed applicant/administrator for 2027, with a recommendation to consider a lead company registered outside Oslo because the Oslo office is often oversubscribed.
- Confirmed interest at the founding meeting, according to Granola: Kinetic Blocks, Lito (Thomas), Hive Autonomy, “and others (4–5 companies)”. A letter of intent is required to apply and was to be circulated to interested parties.
- The December/January event is described as a preparatory activity before formal project start on 1 January 2027 (application deadline cited as 1 December 2026).
